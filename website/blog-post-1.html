Top 5 SUVs for Nigerian Roads

The Nigerian automotive landscape is dominated by utility vehicles, and for good reason. Road conditions, resale value, and parts availability dictate the market.

1. Lexus RX 350

The undisputed king. Comfort, luxury, and unmatched resale value.

2. Toyota Highlander

The practical choice for larger families. Robust suspension and great fuel economy for its size.

3. Toyota Prado

Status symbol meeting rugged capability. Expensive, but invincible.

4. Honda CR-V

Compact, efficient, and reliable. Great for city driving in Lagos and Abuja.

5. Ford Explorer

A growing favorite. Modern tech features and powerful engines, though parts are slightly pricier than Japanese counterparts.
